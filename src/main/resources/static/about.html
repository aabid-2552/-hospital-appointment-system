Built by people tired of waiting on hold.

Meridian started as a scheduling tool for one clinic frustrated with missed calls and double-booked slots. It's since grown into the way thousands of patients find and book care.

What we stand for

Three principles guide every feature we build.

Clarity over complexity

A booking should take three taps, not three phone transfers. We design for the fastest honest path to a confirmed slot.

Trust, by design

Every slot shown is real. If a time is bookable, it's bookable — no overbooking, no surprise cancellations.

Built around people

Doctors keep their own schedules. Patients see only what's actually open. No one manages anyone else's calendar by hand.
